## How I work

- Build status, test results, hands-on checks, and production use are different things. Every project note keeps them separate.
- Ideas for later stay out of the feature list until they exist.
- Private data, credentials, and client material stay out of public pages.

## Background

I grew up in Ethiopia and had just finished my first year of law school when I moved to the US in 2017 and started over. I picked up web development in 2023 to build the first version of an app idea my mother had. I then went through the Per Scholas AWS re/Start program and earned the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner and AWS Certified AI Practitioner certifications. That work grew into DevLookForward, an independent product lab.

Outside the work I read a lot, play soccer, basketball, and ping pong, and sing and play guitar. I have had two official names so far, and my family still uses three more.
